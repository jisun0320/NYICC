1 · Apply & Submit Online

Complete the application on this website, pay the entry fee by card, and submit your performance as an unedited video via a YouTube link. No travel is required to participate in the competition.

For full details, please refer to the Application Guide in the top menu.

2 · Judged by an International Jury

Submissions are evaluated by an international jury composed of internationally acclaimed conductors.

Performances are assessed on musicality, technique, interpretation, expression, and overall artistry. Every applicant is evaluated according to the same artistic standards, with no age divisions.

3 · Winners Perform in Europe in 2027

The winner of each category will be invited to perform with a professional orchestra at a confirmed 2027 concert in Zurich, Vienna, Prague or Eisenstadt.

Each winner receives airfare support (USD 500 within Europe / USD 1,000 from outside Europe), a 4-star hotel for 5 days / 4 nights with breakfast, and airport pickup in the concert city. After the concert, the winner is also provided with a video of the performance for their own reference.

01 · About

About the Competition

Why NYICC exists, who stands behind it, and the partnership that makes five European debuts possible.

NYICC — Opening a New Path to the World Stage

For classical musicians, the opportunity to perform with a professional symphony orchestra and present their artistry on world-class stages is one of their greatest dreams. Yet, even for highly talented musicians, gaining such opportunities can be extremely difficult.

The New York International Concerto Competition (NYICC) was founded with this reality in mind. NYICC seeks to discover exceptional musical talent from around the world — especially gifted artists who have yet to gain international recognition — and provide them with unique opportunities to perform with leading European orchestras.

NYICC is more than a competition that simply selects winners. It is a platform connecting emerging musicians to the international stage, helping them take meaningful steps toward professional careers.

Through its official partnership with IMK (Verein für Internationale Musik und Kulturförderung) in Vienna, NYICC has established a pathway for selected musicians to gain exclusive performance opportunities with professional European orchestras, opening the door to meaningful international concert experiences.

NYICC is not looking for musicians who are already famous. We are looking for the hidden talents who have the potential to shine on the world stage.

From New York to Europe, and from Europe to the world stage — NYICC opens the door to a new journey.

Official Regulations

Competition regulations

Article 1General Provisions

  • The competition is an international online music competition open to musicians of all nationalities.
  • All judging is conducted by online video submission (YouTube link).
  • English is the official language of the competition.

Article 2Categories

  • Piano · Violin · Flute · Cello · Vocal

Article 3Age

  • There is no age limit. All participants are judged by the same artistic standard.

Article 4Application

  • Applications are made through the official website.
  • An entry is valid only when the application form is completed and the entry fee is paid.
  • Entry fees are non-refundable, except where the competition is cancelled by the organizers.

Article 5Video Submission

  • Before performing, the applicant must state aloud their full name, category, the work to be performed, and the recording date.
  • The performer’s hands, instrument and face must be visible on screen throughout. Camera switching or multi-angle compositing is prohibited.
  • The video must be an unedited, continuous recording.
  • Video and audio quality must be sufficient to clearly identify the applicant.
  • Only videos recorded within the designated period (from the opening of applications until the day before the deadline) are accepted.
  • The use of AI-generated content, deepfakes, or synthesized audio is strictly prohibited.

Article 6Judging

  • Performances are evaluated comprehensively on musicality, technique, interpretation, expression and overall artistry.
  • The decisions of the jury are final. No appeals are accepted.

Article 7Prizes

  • The First Prize winner of each category performs a concerto with orchestra at a confirmed 2027 concert (see Schedule & Benefits), and receives an official certificate.
  • Winner support includes airfare support (USD 500 within Europe / USD 1,000 from outside Europe), a 4-star hotel for 5 days / 4 nights with breakfast, airport pickup in the concert city, and a video record of the concert for the winner’s own reference.

Article 8Copyright & Personal Data

  • Applicants consent to the use of submitted videos and photographs for competition promotion.
  • Copyright in the performed works remains with the original rights holders.
  • Personal data is used solely for the administration of the competition.

Article 9Disqualification

  • Submission of false information · violation of video rules · misconduct · copyright infringement · violation of competition regulations.

Article 10Agreement

  • By submitting an application, the applicant is deemed to have read and agreed to all regulations.
  • The organizers may amend or supplement the regulations where necessary.

AppendixRequired Documents

  • Copy of passport or ID
  • Biography of 100–150 words
  • Profile photo
  • Performance program
  • Proof of age, if required

The Halls

Three cities, four historic halls

Tonhalle, Great Hall · Zurich
Zurich, Switzerland

Tonhalle, Great Hall

Cello Winner · 9 April 2027

One of Europe’s most celebrated concert halls, renowned for its warm acoustics and gilded interior. Home to a great tradition of orchestral music since the 19th century, it hosts the NYICC cello winner with the Sofia Philharmonic Orchestra.

ORF TV Hall · Vienna
Vienna, Austria

ORF TV Hall

Violin Winner · 10 April 2027

A broadcast-quality concert hall in the heart of Europe’s musical capital, equipped for professional recording and streaming — a fitting stage for the NYICC violin winner with the Bohuslav Martinů Philharmonic Orchestra.

Smetana Hall · Prague
Prague, Czech Republic

Smetana Hall

Vocal Winner · 24 April  |  Piano Winner · 8 May 2027

The magnificent Art Nouveau hall inside Prague’s Municipal House, one of the great ceremonial concert spaces of Central Europe. It hosts two NYICC concerts — vocal and piano — with the North Czech Philharmonic Orchestra.

Haydn Hall · Eisenstadt
Eisenstadt, Austria

Haydn Hall

Flute Winner · 19 June 2027

Named after the composer who worked here, the Haydn Hall in Schloss Esterházy — with its Baroque frescoes and celebrated acoustics — is one of the world’s most beautiful concert halls, hosting over a hundred concerts each year. It is the stage for the NYICC flute winner with the Bohuslav Martinů Philharmonic Orchestra.
